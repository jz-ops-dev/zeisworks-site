# Local Search Visibility Checklist
## 25 things every local service business should have locked down

**zeisWorks · zeisworks.com · (720) 650-2447**

---

### SECTION 1: Google Business Profile (GBP)

- [ ] Claimed and verified your Google Business Profile
- [ ] Business name matches exactly what's on your signage and website
- [ ] Primary category is the most specific match for your core service
- [ ] At least 2–3 secondary categories added
- [ ] Business description written (750 chars) with primary keyword in first sentence
- [ ] All service areas listed (if you serve multiple cities or zip codes)
- [ ] Hours are accurate and holiday hours are updated
- [ ] At least 10 photos uploaded (interior, exterior, team, work samples)
- [ ] Products or services section populated with descriptions and prices
- [ ] Q&A section seeded with your 5 most common customer questions

---

### SECTION 2: Reviews

- [ ] You have a direct review link ready to send customers
- [ ] You have a repeatable process for requesting reviews after every job
- [ ] You respond to every review — positive and negative
- [ ] You have at least 10 Google reviews (20+ is competitive in most markets)
- [ ] Your average rating is 4.5 or higher

---

### SECTION 3: Citation Consistency

- [ ] Your business name, address, and phone (NAP) are identical across all directories
- [ ] You're listed on the top 10 directories: Google, Yelp, Facebook, BBB, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Foursquare, Angi, HomeAdvisor, Yellow Pages
- [ ] No duplicate listings exist on Google or Yelp
- [ ] Your website URL is consistent across all listings

---

### SECTION 4: Website Basics

- [ ] Your homepage has your city/service area in the title tag and H1
- [ ] You have a dedicated page for each core service
- [ ] Your phone number is clickable (tel: link) on mobile
- [ ] Your site loads in under 3 seconds on mobile
- [ ] You have a Google Maps embed on your contact page

---

### SECTION 5: Competitive Positioning

- [ ] You know who your top 3 local competitors are in Google Maps
- [ ] You've reviewed their GBP profiles to see what they're doing differently
- [ ] You know which keywords you're ranking for vs. not ranking for
- [ ] You're tracking your local pack position at least monthly

---

- 18–22 checked: You have clear gaps. Prioritize GBP completeness and citation cleanup.
